CONFLICT TIMELINE

🔴 FEBRUARY 27, 2026
PAKISTAN DECLARES "OPEN WAR" - AIRSTRIKES ON KABUL & KANDAHAR
Defense Minister Khawaja Asif declares "open war" between Pakistan and Afghan Taliban. Pakistan launches coordinated airstrikes on Kabul, Kandahar, and Paktia provinces. Operation "Ghazab Lil Haq" (Righteous Fury) targets Taliban brigade headquarters, weapons depots, and border installations. Pakistan claims to have destroyed 83 Taliban posts, captured 17 others, and destroyed 171 tanks.
🔴 FEBRUARY 26, 2026
AFGHAN TALIBAN LAUNCHES CROSS-BORDER OFFENSIVE
Taliban forces conduct large-scale attacks against Pakistani military posts along Durand Line. Afghan sources claim 10 Pakistani soldiers killed and 13 outposts captured. Taliban spokesperson Zabihullah Mujahid confirms offensive in retaliation for Pakistani airstrikes in Nangarhar and Paktia.
🔴 MARCH 1, 2026
PAKISTAN HITS BAGRAM AIRFIELD
Pakistan's information minister Attaullah Tarar reveals 46 locations across Afghanistan hit by airstrikes since operation began, including historic Bagram Airfield. Pakistan continues operations on Prime Minister's orders.
⚠️ FEBRUARY 21, 2026
PAKISTAN AIRSTRIKES BEGIN - OPERATION AGAINST TTP HIDEOUTS
Pakistan Air Force conducts airstrikes on alleged TTP camps in Nangarhar, Paktika, and Khost provinces. Pakistan reports 80+ militants killed; Afghanistan confirms 18 civilian deaths including 11 children in Bihsud District. Retaliation for suicide bombing at Shiite mosque in Islamabad (36 killed) and other terror attacks.
⚠️ FEBRUARY 6-16, 2026
TERROR ATTACKS TRIGGER ESCALATION
Series of terror attacks across Pakistan: Suicide bombing at Shia mosque in Islamabad kills 36 people. Attack on checkpoint in Bajaur kills 11 soldiers and 1 child. Balochistan Liberation Army (BLA) attacks from January 29-February 5. Pakistan issues démarche to Afghan ambassador, warns of military action.
🟡 OCTOBER 2025
MAJOR ESCALATION & CEASEFIRE
October 9: Pakistan airstrikes in Kabul, Khost, Jalalabad targeting TTP leader Noor Wali Mehsud (survives). October 11-12: Afghan Taliban retaliatory attacks on Pakistani posts. October 15: Heavy fighting in Spin Boldak, Kandahar with 12+ Afghan civilian deaths. October 19: Qatar and Turkey-mediated ceasefire agreed. Ceasefire breaks down as TTP continues attacks.

KEY CONFLICT LOCATIONS

🇦🇫 KABUL
Capital of Afghanistan. Pakistan airstrikes on February 21 & 27, 2026. Abdul Haq Square targeted.
🇦🇫 KANDAHAR
Taliban supreme leader Hibatullah Akhundzada base. Major Pakistan airstrike target. February 27 strikes hit army headquarters.
🇦🇫 BAGRAM AIRFIELD
Historic military base. Struck by Pakistan on March 1, 2026. Part of Operation "Ghazab Lil Haq".
🇦🇫 NANGARHAR PROVINCE
Major conflict zone. Pakistan airstrikes February 21. Civilian casualties reported in Bihsud District. TTP hideouts targeted.
🇦🇫 KHOST PROVINCE
Eastern border region. Pakistan airstrikes in October 2025 and February 2026. TTP militia camps reported.
🇦🇫 PAKTIKA PROVINCE
Border region with Pakistan. December 2024 & February 2026 airstrikes. TTP stronghold.
🇦🇫 SPIN BOLDAK (KANDAHAR)
Border crossing with Pakistan. October 15, 2025: Heavy fighting, 12+ civilians killed, 122+ wounded. Taliban claims captured posts.
🇵🇰 KUNAR PROVINCE (BORDER)
Pakistan-Afghanistan border. Cross-border attacks, mortar fire, RPG strikes. January 2026: RPG hit football field in North Waziristan, 3 wounded.
🇵🇰 KHYBER PAKHTUNKHWA
Pakistani province bordering Afghanistan. Heavy TTP attack base. Border clashes ongoing. Chitral, Khyber, Mohmand, Kurram, Bajaur sectors active.
🇵🇰 ISLAMABAD
Pakistan capital. February 6: Suicide bombing at Shia mosque kills 36 people. Major trigger for military response.
🇵🇰 BAJAUR
Border district. February 16: TTP attack kills 11 soldiers and 1 child. Ongoing military operations.
🇦🇫 DURAND LINE
2,610 km border between Pakistan and Afghanistan. Disputed colonial-era demarcation. 75+ clashes since 2021. Core of conflict.
